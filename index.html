About Me

I am currently a final year Ph.D. student in the Department of Electronic Engineering, Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), supervised by Prof. Yixuan Yuan. Before that, I received B. Eng. degree in Electronic Information Engineering from the Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) in 2021. During my Ph.D. studies, I was fortunate to work with Dr. Zhenwei Wang and Dr. Tengfei Wang from Tencent Hunyuan, 3D Center, World Model Team.
