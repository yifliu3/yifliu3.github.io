Yifan Liu

I am a Researcher at Tencent HY, working with Dr. Heng Wang in the Video World Foundation Model Team. Previously, I received my Ph.D. in Electronic Engineering from the Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Yixuan Yuan. During my Ph.D., I have been fortunate to work with Dr. Zhenwei Wang and Dr. Tengfei Wang at the Tencent HY 3D.

My research focuses on video generation, world models, and 3D vision, with an emphasis on building practical products for generation and understanding in real-world applications.

I'm always open to related discussions and collaborations. If you are interested in my work, please feel free to contact me.
